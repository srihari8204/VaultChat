Delete your crazzychat account

Two ways to do it — in the app, or by writing to us.

In the app — immediate

  1. Open crazzychat and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete my account.
  3. Type the number (or email) on the account, then tap Delete my account and confirm. That prompt is the point of no return.

Deletion takes effect straight away. Every device signed into the account is signed out and cannot sign back in.

By request — if you cannot open the app

If you have lost the device or cannot get past the lock screen, email privacy@corefinite.com from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my crazzychat account.

We will confirm the request is genuine before acting on it — an account deletion that anyone could trigger for someone else would be a security hole of its own — and we will complete it within 30 days, usually far sooner.

This cannot be undone. There is no grace period and no restore path. We do not keep a copy of your encryption keys, so even we cannot bring the account back or read anything that was in it.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Messages you sent to other people. Their copy is on their device, encrypted with keys we have never held — it is theirs, and deleting your account does not reach into someone else's conversation. Our own server-side copy is erased on the normal schedule once every device has received it.
  • An empty placeholder record. A row with no personal data in it stays behind so that other people's conversations, groups, and records do not break. It holds nothing that identifies you.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires and no longer.

Before you go

If you only wanted to stop being findable or visible, you may not need to delete anything. crazzychat has Ghost Mode, last-seen and read-receipt controls, per-chat locks, and blocking — all in Settings.
