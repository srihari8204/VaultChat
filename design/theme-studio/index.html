Themes
Why do icons need an extra step?

An app can't silently replace the icons on your home screen — the system asks you to confirm. Facet prepares every icon in your theme, then hands them to your phone's own installation step. We tell you which parts apply instantly and which need that step before you tap Apply.

Can I undo an applied theme?

Yes. Your previous theme stays in Library under Recently used. Applying it again restores everything Facet changed.

Do my AI prompts leave my phone?

No. Generation runs on your device, which is also why it works offline and returns instantly.

What happens to themes I created if I cancel Facet+?

They stay yours. Everything you made and saved remains in your Library; only premium content and unlimited AI generation stop.
