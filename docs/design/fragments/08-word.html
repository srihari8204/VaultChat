2. Market Opportunity

Section owner: Maya Chen · reviewed by Finance 03 Aug 2026

Enterprise teams now exchange more than 60% of their working documents inside chat tools that were never designed to hold them. A file leaves the conversation the moment it is opened — handed to a desktop application, duplicated into a downloads folder, and stripped of every access control the sender intended. Atlas closes that gap by keeping the document, the conversation, and the policy in a single sealed surface.

Our bottom-up model, built from the 2026 Secure Collaboration Benchmark and 41 buyer interviews, places the combined addressable market at $4.6B by FY28, growing 19% annually as regulated industries retire legacy intranet and managed-file-transfer tooling.

Three procurement signals make the timing favourable. Security review now precedes tooling selection in 71% of target accounts; consolidation budgets reward suites that replace two or more point tools; and data-residency mandates coming into force across the EU and India fall inside the plan horizon.

2.2 Where Atlas wins first

The beachhead is the regulated mid-market: firms of 200–2,000 seats in financial services, health, and legal that already mandate end-to-end encryption for messaging, yet still route documents through unmanaged local viewers. For these buyers the purchase is a security decision first and a productivity decision second — a sequencing Atlas's sealed viewer is uniquely positioned to satisfy.
