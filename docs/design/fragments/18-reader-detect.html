Team — apologies in advance, this is a long one. We're changing how Atlas ships. The plan we signed off in June assumed a single launch day: press, pricing and product landing together on September 2. After three weeks of dogfooding and one genuinely humbling incident review, I no longer believe a big-bang launch is the responsible move. This message replaces the June plan; read it before Thursday's review, because everything below freezes after that.

Two things changed. First, the checkout migration is running a week behind, and pretending otherwise only moves the slip somewhere more expensive. Second, the August 1 incident review showed that our rollback path under load was theory, not practice — the flag service is fixed now, but the fix has never seen real traffic. A staged rollout gives it three chances to fail safely…
