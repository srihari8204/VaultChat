2. Market Opportunity

Section owner: Maya Chen · reviewed by Finance 03 Aug 2026

Enterprise teams now exchange more than 60% of their working documents inside chat tools that were never designed to hold them. A file leaves the conversation the moment it is opened — handed to another application, copied into a downloads folder, and stripped of every control the sender intended.

Our bottom-up model, built from the 2026 Secure Collaboration Benchmark and 41 buyer interviews, places the combined addressable market at $4.6B by FY28, growing 19% a year as regulated industries retire legacy file-transfer tooling.
