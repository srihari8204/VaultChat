The Atlas Rollout Plan

MW Marcus Webb 6 min read · 3 pages · 1,412 words

We're changing how Atlas ships. The June plan assumed one launch day — press, pricing and product landing on September 2. After three weeks of dogfooding, that is no longer responsible.

Why we're changing it

Checkout is a week behind, and pretending otherwise only moves the slip somewhere more expensive. If partner-cohort usage drops below 60%, we pause the GA date — no meeting, no debate.

  • Phases replace the launch day — each with an owner and a rehearsed rollback.
  • Pricing ships dark in Phase 1 — we meter real usage for two weeks first.
  • No phase starts on a Friday. This is now written down.
