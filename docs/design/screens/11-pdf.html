3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 Fees. Client shall pay Provider the fees set out in Schedule B. Unless expressly stated otherwise, all fees are exclusive of applicable taxes and are payable in EUR within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice.
3.2 Renewal Adjustment. Upon each renewal term, Provider may increase the recurring fees by no more than the lesser of (a) four and one-half percent (4.5%) or (b) the applicable CPI change for the preceding twelve (12) months, upon written notice at least sixty (60) days prior to renewal.
3.3 Late Payment. Amounts more than fifteen (15) days overdue accrue interest at 1.5% per month, and Provider may apply a reinstatement uplift of 4.5% to accounts suspended for non-payment.
