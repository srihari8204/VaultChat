VaultChat Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

The short version. Your messages, calls, and file transfers are end-to-end encrypted — the keys live on your device and we cannot read them, even if we wanted to or were compelled to try. We keep as little as we can about you, we encrypt what we do keep, we never sell it, and there are no ads in VaultChat.

This policy explains what VaultChat collects, why, how long it stays, and what you can do about it. It covers the VaultChat mobile app and the services behind it, operated by Corefinite Tech Solutions LLP ("we", "us").

1. What is end-to-end encrypted

The following never reach us in a form we can read. They are encrypted on your device with keys that only you and the people you are talking to hold:

  • One-to-one messages — X3DH key agreement and a Double Ratchet, so each message uses a fresh key.
  • Group messages — encrypted to a sender key shared only among members.
  • Voice and video calls — the call's media key is sealed to each participant. Our media servers forward packets they cannot decrypt.
  • Attachments and media — encrypted before upload; our storage holds ciphertext and never the key.
  • Direct file transfers — encrypted end to end whether they travel over your local network, directly between devices, or via a relay.
  • Encrypted notes and your encrypted backup — protected by a key derived from a secret you set, which we do not hold.

Because of this, we cannot recover your message history for you, and we cannot hand your message content to anyone else. That is the design, not a policy choice we could reverse.

3. Location, and Family Space

VaultChat only collects location if you turn on Family Space location sharing. It is off until you switch it on, and switching it off stops collection.

  • Your location is shared only with members of the family circle you joined — never publicly, never with other VaultChat users, never with advertisers.
  • Collection continues in the background while sharing is on, so your circle can see you are safe and so trips and safe-zone alerts work. Android shows a persistent notification while this is running.
  • Update frequency adapts to whether you are moving, to save battery.
  • For "measure me from" reference places, your device computes the distance and publishes only that number. The place itself stays on your device.
  • Physical-activity recognition is used only to tell whether you are moving, so updates can slow down when you are still.
  • You can stop sharing at any time, and history can be cleared from the app.

4. How long we keep things

Message retention is tied to delivery, not to a clock:

  • Once every device in a conversation has received a message, our copy of its ciphertext is erased after a short grace period. Your device keeps the message locally; that copy is the real one.
  • An undelivered message is kept until it can be delivered. We are the only copy that can still reach a phone that has been off for a month, so we do not discard it on a timer.
  • Encrypted attachments follow the same delivery-based rule.
  • Account records are kept until you delete your account (section 6).
  • Crash reports are retained on a rolling basis for debugging.

5. Who we share with

We share personal data with no one for their own purposes. We rely on a small number of processors who handle data only on our instructions:

  • Hosting — our servers, which hold the encrypted data described above.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver push notifications.
  • Sentry — crash diagnostics.

We may disclose information if we are legally required to. Because of end-to-end encryption, what we are technically able to produce is limited to the account and metadata described in section 2 — never message, call, or file content.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account — in the app: Settings → Delete account. Or use the web form at /delete-account. See that page for exactly what is removed.
  • See and revoke sessions — Settings shows every signed-in device; you can sign any of them out.
  • Access and correction — your profile is editable in the app; contact us for anything else.
  • Withdraw consent — every permission (contacts, location, camera, microphone, notifications) can be revoked in Android Settings, and location sharing can be switched off inside the app. Features that need a permission stop working; the rest of the app continues.
  • Privacy controls — read receipts, last-seen, ghost mode, and per-chat locks are all in the app.

7. Children

VaultChat is not for children under 13, and the app refuses to create an account for a date of birth under that age. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

Alongside end-to-end encryption: personal fields are encrypted at rest, credentials are stored only as Argon2id hashes, all traffic uses TLS, sign-in is protected by rate limiting and lockouts, and device backup of app data is disabled so your keys cannot be extracted from a device backup.

No system is perfect. If you find a security problem, please report it to us before disclosing it publicly — see the contact below.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for anything significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
